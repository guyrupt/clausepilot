# PacificShield Comprehensive Travel Inconvenience Policy (2026 Taiwan Outbound Edition)
**Form Code:** `PS-TW-2026-OUTBOUND` | **Underwriter:** PacificShield Marine & General Insurance Co., Ltd. (Taipei Head Office) | **Settlement Currency:** USD (United States Dollars)

---

## Article 15 — Policy Scope, Definitions & Currency Basis (契約範圍、名詞定義與匯率基準)

1. **Insured Person (被保險人):** A Taiwan resident named on the Policy Declarations whose international round-trip or multi-city itinerary originates from the Home Country.
2. **Home Country (本國／居住地國):** The Republic of China (Taiwan), expressly encompassing Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport (`TPE`), Taipei Songshan Airport (`TSA`), Taichung International Airport (`RMQ`), and Kaohsiung International Airport (`KHH`). Any flight leg terminating at a Home Country airport constitutes a **Home-Country Return Leg (返國入境航段)**.
3. **Abroad / Overseas (海外／國外地區):** Any sovereign jurisdiction or territory outside the Home Country (including Japan `NRT`/`HND`/`KIX`, Hong Kong `HKG`, Singapore `SIN`, United Kingdom `LHR`, and Europe/North America).
4. **Publicly Announced Event (已公開宣布之突發事故):** An official tropical cyclone or typhoon sea/land warning issued by the Taiwan Central Weather Administration (`CWA` 中央氣象署) or Japan Meteorological Agency (`JMA`), an official airport closure NOTAM, or a public carrier labor strike notice bearing an official publication timestamp.
5. **Currency Conversion Basis:** Foreign currency receipts (`JPY`, `TWD`, `HKD`, `GBP`, `EUR`) are settled in USD at the official carrier/merchant dual-currency settlement rate printed on the tax invoice or the Bank of Taiwan spot selling rate on the incident date (reference benchmark: `1 USD = 150 JPY`; `1 USD = 32.0 TWD`).

## Article 16 — Period of Insurance & Automatic Weather Delay Extension (保險期間與天候延誤自動延長)

Coverage attaches at the Policy Purchase Timestamp shown on the Declarations (with Travel Inconvenience benefits effective upon check-in or scheduled departure) and terminates upon arrival at the Home Country airport or expiry of the scheduled policy period. If an Insured Person's scheduled return flight back to the Home Country (e.g., `NRT -> TPE`) is delayed at an overseas departure airport due to inclement weather, typhoon closure of the destination airport, or mechanical breakdown, this Policy **automatically extends without additional premium for up to seventy-two (72) hours** until the Insured completes arrival on the first available recovery flight.

## Article 18 — Trip Cancellation Coverage (旅程取消保險金)

The Insurer shall indemnify the Insured up to **USD $3,000.00** for non-refundable, prepaid passenger transport tickets and hotel deposits forfeited prior to outbound departure from the Home Country when cancellation is directly and necessarily caused by:
- Death, emergency inpatient hospitalization, or critical injury of the Insured, spouse, parent, or child within seven (7) days prior to departure;
- Jury summons, subpoena, or mandatory quarantine order issued individually to the Insured; or
- Catastrophic earthquake, volcanic eruption, or tsunami at the scheduled overseas destination rendering booked accommodation officially uninhabitable.
Indemnification under Article 18 is subject to the conditions and disqualifications set forth in Article 19.

## Article 19 — Trip Cancellation Exclusions (旅程取消特別不保事項)

Notwithstanding Article 18, no Trip Cancellation benefit shall be payable for losses arising directly or indirectly from:
1. Personal disinclination to travel, business schedule changes, or career obligations;
2. Financial default or bankruptcy of a travel agency, tour operator, or unbonded ticket consolidator;
3. Any weather event, strike, or civil disturbance that constituted a Publicly Announced Event prior to the Policy Purchase Timestamp; or
4. Failure to notify the carrier or hotel provider within forty-eight (48) hours of the triggering event to mitigate forfeiture penalties.

## Article 20 — Trip Interruption & Curtailment Coverage (旅程更改與縮短行程保險)

If, after departing the Home Country, the Insured must curtail an ongoing overseas journey and return urgently to the Home Country due to the unexpected death or ICU hospitalization of an immediate family member in Taiwan, or an official Ministry of Foreign Affairs (`MOFA`) Red Travel Alert issued after departure, the Insurer shall reimburse necessary additional economy-class return airfare and forfeited hotel nights up to **USD $1,500.00**.

## Article 21 — Flight Delay Fixed Benefit (班機延誤定額給付保險金)

If a Scheduled Common Carrier flight on which the Insured holds a confirmed reservation (including an outbound flight, an international transit flight, or an inbound return flight departing an overseas airport such as `NRT -> TPE`) is delayed by **six (6) consecutive hours or more** measured from the scheduled departure time to actual departure time (or scheduled arrival to actual arrival) due to inclement weather, typhoon airport closure, air traffic control flow restrictions, or sudden aircraft mechanical malfunction, the Insurer shall pay a **Fixed Inconvenience Benefit (定額給付)** without requiring meal/hotel receipts:
- **Delay of 6 hours to under 12 hours:** **USD $250.00** fixed lump-sum benefit.
- **Delay of 12 hours or more (or overnight cancellation rebooked next day >= 12h):** **USD $500.00** maximum fixed benefit per policy period.

Payment under Article 21 is strictly governed by the conjunction ticket rules in Section 21.4 and the mandatory exclusions in Article 22. Where a covered delay of 6 hours or more strands the Insured **overnight outside the Home Country**, this Fixed Benefit is payable **in addition to** eligible receipted expenses under Section 24.1.

### Section 21.4 — Single-PNR Conjunction Ticket Rule & Separate-Ticket Escalation (聯程單一訂位代號條款與分開購票轉介資深理賠師審查)

1. **Single-PNR Requirement for Missed Connections:** Automated Tier-1 benefit eligibility under Article 21 and Article 23 for missed onward flight connections applies **exclusively** when both the incoming feeder flight and the onward connecting flight were issued together under a **Single Passenger Name Record (Single PNR / Conjunction Ticket)** satisfying the airline's published Minimum Connecting Time (`MCT`).
2. **Mandatory Escalation on Separate Tickets / Split PNRs (分開購票／雙PNR強制轉介條款):** Where an Insured books consecutive flights across **two separate tickets or distinct PNRs** (Self-Transfer / Split-PNR itinerary, e.g., Carrier A `PNR #1` connecting to Carrier B `PNR #2`) and misses the second flight following a first-leg delay of **under 6 hours**, **frontline Tier-1 support clerks have ZERO authority to approve or reject walk-up rebooking airfare**.
3. **Required Adjudication Action (`escalate_senior`):** Any claim involving separate PNRs / split tickets MUST be placed on hold with **USD $0.00 immediate clerk payout** and **escalated to a Senior Licensed Claims Adjuster (`escalate_senior`)** for manual underwriting review of interline protection waivers, MCT buffer adequacy, and discretionary ex-gratia sub-limits.

## Article 22 — Flight Delay Exclusions: Pre-Existing Typhoon Warning & First Replacement Flight (班機延誤特別不保事項)

No benefit shall be payable under Article 21, Article 23, or Section 24.1 if any of the following exclusions apply:
1. **Section 22.1 — Pre-Existing Typhoon, Weather, or Strike Warning Before Policy Purchase (投保前已發布颱風警報或罷工預告之除外):** Claims caused by a named typhoon, tropical storm, or carrier labor strike are **excluded** IF AND ONLY IF the official warning (such as a `CWA` Central Weather Administration Typhoon Warning or `JMA` Typhoon Advisory) or strike announcement was **publicly issued prior to the exact Policy Purchase Timestamp**. *(Clarification: If the Insured purchased the Policy BEFORE the official typhoon warning publication timestamp, coverage remains 100% valid and enforceable.)*
2. **Section 22.2 — Declining First Available Replacement Flight (拒絕搭乘最早可替代航班之除外):** Any delay duration or overnight expense incurred after the Insured voluntarily declines, misses, or elects a later departure than the **first available replacement or recovery flight** offered by the operating carrier is strictly excluded, unless prevented by documented inpatient medical emergency.

## Article 23 — Missed Flight Connection Rebooking Coverage (班機錯過轉機改票費用保險)

When an Insured holding a **Single-PNR Conjunction Ticket** misses a confirmed international onward flight at an overseas transit hub due to a late-arriving incoming scheduled flight caused by weather or mechanical delay (and the carrier cannot provide an alternative onward flight within 6 hours), the Insurer reimburses reasonable economy-class rebooking fare differences and transit hotel charges up to **USD $1,500.00**, subject strictly to Section 21.4 and Article 22. Self-transfer itineraries booked on separate PNRs are excluded from Tier-1 automated approval and must follow the Senior Adjuster escalation protocol in Section 21.4.

## Article 24 — Overnight Delay Necessary Expenses Abroad (海外過夜延誤必要住宿與膳食費用保險)

### Section 24.1 — Cumulative Overnight Accommodation & Meal Reimbursement Abroad (海外滯留過夜必要住宿與餐飲實支實付 — 得與第21條定額並領)

When a covered flight delay of **six (6) consecutive hours or more** under Article 21 occurs while the Insured is **outside the Home Country** (expressly including when a return flight back to Taiwan such as `NRT -> TPE` is stranded overnight at the overseas departure city due to typhoon closure of Taipei Taoyuan Airport) and necessitates an unplanned overnight stay abroad, the Insurer shall reimburse actual, reasonable, itemized receipted expenses for:
1. **Overnight Hotel Accommodation (海外過夜飯店住宿費)** in the city of stranding; and
2. **Necessary Restaurant Meals & Non-Alcoholic Refreshments (必要餐飲膳食／定食)** consumed during the delay period prior to boarding the recovery flight,
up to a combined aggregate limit of **USD $500.00** per event.

**Explicit Cumulation Rule:** Benefits payable under Section 24.1 are **CUMULATIVE with (paid in full addition to)** the USD $250.00 Flight Delay Fixed Benefit under Article 21, provided the policy preconditions in Article 22 are satisfied and every receipted line item complies strictly with the sub-limits and item exclusions in Section 24.3.

### Section 24.2 — Overseas Airport-to-Hotel Ground Transportation (海外機場往返過夜飯店交通費)

Where an Insured qualifies for overnight accommodation reimbursement under Section 24.1, the Insurer also reimburses receipted public railway (`Narita Express` / `Skyliner`), airport limousine bus, or licensed metered taxi fare directly between the overseas airport and the overnight hotel up to **USD $60.00** round-trip, subject to Section 24.3 documentation rules.

### Section 24.3 — Sub-Limits & Strictly Non-Covered Items: $150/Night Hotel Cap & Alcohol Exclusion (住宿每晚最高150美元限額及酒類菸品除外條款)

All claims under Section 24.1 and Article 24 are subject to the following **mandatory per-item sub-limits and exclusions**, which claims personnel must enforce line-by-line on itemized receipts:
1. **Hotel Accommodation Sub-Limit (`USD $150.00 / night` Cap):** Reimbursement for overnight hotel lodging (inclusive of municipal accommodation tax and service charges) is **strictly capped at a maximum sub-limit of USD $150.00 per night** per Insured Person. Where an Insured submits a hotel invoice exceeding USD $150.00 per night (for example, `¥36,000 / USD $240.00` at a Tokyo hotel), the item is **NOT denied in full**, but **MUST be capped at exactly USD $150.00 (`status: "capped"`)**, disallowing the excess above USD $150.00.
2. **Strict Exclusion of Alcoholic Beverages, Tobacco & Duty-Free Goods (`USD $0.00` Rejected):** The Insurer **strictly excludes and pays USD $0.00** for any **alcoholic beverages of any kind** — expressly including **draft beer (`生ビール`), bottled beer (`ビール`), sake (`日本酒`), shochu, wine, spirits, highballs, or hotel minibar alcohol** — as well as tobacco/vape products, spa/massage charges, entertainment, souvenirs, and duty-free luxury goods. When alcoholic beverages (`生ビール` / draft beer) appear on the same restaurant or izakaya receipt (`居酒屋領収書`) as a covered food meal (`定食` / set dinner), **only the alcoholic beverage line item is rejected (`USD $0.00`, `status: "rejected"`)**, while the non-alcoholic meal line item remains payable in full under Section 24.1.

## Article 25 — Loss of Travel Documents Abroad (海外旅行文件遺失重置費用)

If the Insured's Republic of China passport or required destination entry visa is lost or stolen while overseas (supported by a local police report filed within 24 hours), the Insurer reimburses official consular emergency travel document replacement fees and necessary local transport to the Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office (`TECRO`) up to **USD $300.00**.

## Article 26 — Baggage Delay Emergency Essentials Coverage (行李延誤緊急日用品與衣物實支實付保險)

If the Insured's checked accompanied baggage, duly tagged and Receipted by a Scheduled Common Carrier, is delayed, misrouted, or temporarily misplaced for **six (6) consecutive hours or more** following the Insured's actual arrival at a scheduled destination airport, the Insurer shall reimburse actual, itemized receipted purchases of **emergency essential clothing, underwear, and basic toiletries** incurred within seventy-two (72) hours of arrival, up to **USD $400.00** per occurrence, supported by the carrier's official Property Irregularity Report (`PIR`).

## Article 27 — Baggage Delay Exclusions (行李延誤特別不保事項)

### Article 27.1 — Customs Detention, Cargo & Non-Essential Merchandise Exclusion (海關扣留與非必要精品除外)

Notwithstanding Article 26, no reimbursement shall be made for baggage delayed due to customs inspection, agricultural quarantine, or government confiscation, nor for electronics, cosmetics/perfume, jewelry, suitcases, or items shipped separately under air waybill cargo freight.

### Article 27.2 — Inbound Home-Country Arrival Baggage Exclusion (返國抵達本國機場之行李延誤除外條款)

**Mandatory Home-Airport Exclusion (Applicable to Article 26):** Notwithstanding Article 26, **ZERO benefit (`USD $0.00`, full denial)** is payable when checked baggage is delayed, misdirected, or delivered late **upon the Insured's return arrival at any airport within their Home Country / Country of Residence** (expressly including return flights landing at **Taipei Taoyuan `TPE`, Taipei Songshan `TSA`, or Kaohsiung `KHH`**, such as an `NRT -> TPE` return leg), **regardless of whether the baggage delay exceeds 6 hours or 15 hours**. Because the Insured has returned to their Home Country where domestic wardrobe and residence essentials are deemed available, all clothing or toiletry receipts (e.g., Uniqlo apparel or pharmacy toiletries purchased in Taipei after landing) MUST be **100% denied (`USD $0.00`)** under Article 27.2.
